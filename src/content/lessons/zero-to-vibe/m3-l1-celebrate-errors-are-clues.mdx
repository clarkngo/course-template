## 🎉 Celebrate: Errors Are Clues, Not Verdicts

At some point — maybe already — your app is going to fully break. Not "the color's wrong," but a blank screen, or a wall of red text, or a message full of words you don't recognize.

For most beginners, that moment feels like a verdict: *"I've hit the edge of what I can do without really learning to code."* It isn't. It's information. A specific, useful clue about exactly one thing that needs to change — nothing more.

**An error message is the AI telling you precisely where to look next.** That's not a punishment for doing something wrong. It's closer to a smoke detector: unpleasant to hear, genuinely useful, and not something you're expected to interpret on your own — you just report it.

> This module's celebration moment: **the scarier the error looks, the more precisely it's telling you what to fix. Ugly text is not the same as an unsolvable problem.**

You're about to prove this by breaking your own app on purpose.
