## 🎉 Rejoice: You Already Won

Here's the guiding idea for this whole module, and honestly, for this whole course:

**You get to feel like a builder before you understand how building works.**

Most classes do it backwards. They hand you theory for weeks, and only at the very end do you get to make something real. By the time you finally build anything, half the class has already decided "this isn't for me" and quit.

This course flips that. In the very next lesson — before any lecture, before any vocabulary, before anything that looks like "learning to code" — you are going to build a real, working app. It will run in a browser. You'll be able to show it to someone. That happens first.

The lecture comes *after*. Once you've already felt what it's like to describe something and watch it come to life, the explanation of *why* it worked will actually mean something to you, instead of being abstract theory you have to take on faith.

> That's the rejoice moment of Module 1: **you don't have to earn the win. You get to have it first, and then understand it.**

You already know why this works — the flipped Bloom's Taxonomy model from Module 0. This is that model, in motion, for the first time.
