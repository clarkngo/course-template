## 🎉 Rejoice, Revisited

Go back to Module 1's opening line: *you don't have to earn the win, you get to have it first, and then understand it.*

You just lived that sentence. You built an app before you knew a single technical term. Then you learned the terms, and they made sense immediately, because you already had something real to attach them to.

That's the shape of every module in this course. Doing first, understanding second, and a check on both ends to prove the gap closed.
