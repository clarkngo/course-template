## 🎉 Rejoice: Taste Is a Skill You Already Have

Here's something you've been doing your entire life without noticing: judging whether something is well-made. You know within seconds whether a website feels confusing or clear. You know when a form is annoying to fill out. You know when a button doesn't look clickable, even if you couldn't explain the design rule behind why.

That instinct is called **taste**, and you already have it — years of it, built from being a user of thousands of apps and websites. What this module does is *not* teach you taste from zero. It teaches you to trust the taste you already have, and translate it into instructions an AI can act on.

**You don't need a design degree to know when something feels off. You need permission to say so, specifically.**

> This module's rejoice moment: **you've been training for this module since the first time you got frustrated with a confusing app. That frustration was taste, working correctly.**
