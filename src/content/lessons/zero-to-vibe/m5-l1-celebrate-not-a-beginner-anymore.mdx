## 🎉 Celebrate: You're Not a Beginner Anymore

Look at what you've already done, in order:

- **Module 1:** Built a working app from a plain-English description, before any lecture
- **Module 2:** Ran the describe-test-refine loop, three times, on purpose
- **Module 3:** Broke your own app twice, and fixed it twice, by reporting clearly instead of debugging code
- **Module 4:** Judged your own work through a stranger's eyes, and directed a real fix

That's not a beginner's résumé. That's the actual, complete loop that every vibe coder — including ones doing this professionally — runs on every project, big or small. You've just run it on something small.

**This module doesn't teach you a new skill. It asks you to use all four at once, on something you choose, and then let someone else actually use it.**

> This module's celebration moment: **there is no "final boss" of vibe coding waiting for you after this. What you've already practiced is the whole game, played at a bigger size.**
