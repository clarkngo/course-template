## 🎉 Celebrate, Revisited

Think back to how a wall of red error text felt before this module, versus how it felt when you triggered it on purpose last lesson. If it went from *dread* to *mild curiosity* — that's the actual shift this module was built to produce. Errors were never a verdict on your ability. They were always just clues, waiting to be reported clearly.
