## 🎉 Celebrate: Nothing Is Ever Broken, Only Unfinished

Your Module 1 app almost certainly wasn't perfect. Maybe the button didn't do quite what you pictured, or the colors were wrong, or a fact never showed up. If you felt a flicker of *"I did this wrong"* — that reaction is the thing this module exists to retrain.

**A rough first version isn't a mistake to feel bad about. It's raw material.**

Every app you'll ever build with AI starts life a little bit wrong. That's not a sign you described it badly — it's just what a first draft is, in any medium. A first draft of an email is wrong. A first sketch is wrong. The wrongness isn't the failure state; it's step one of a process that has more steps.

The professionals who do this for a living aren't running some fundamentally different, more careful process than you did in Module 1. They're running the exact same loop, more times, on bigger ideas. That's the entire skill gap between a beginner and an expert here — reps through the loop, not secret knowledge.

> This module's celebration moment: **the AI getting it "wrong" the first time isn't a problem to solve. It's the process working exactly as intended.**
