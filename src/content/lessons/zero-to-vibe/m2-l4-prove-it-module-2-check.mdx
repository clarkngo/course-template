## 🎉 Celebrate, Revisited

Look back at how you reacted the first time your Module 1 app didn't quite work, versus how you reacted during Round 1 of this module. If the second time felt more like *"okay, let's fix this"* and less like *"I did something wrong"* — that shift is the actual point of this module. The loop isn't a fallback for when things go badly. It's the normal, expected shape of building anything.
