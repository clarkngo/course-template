## 🧰 What You Actually Need

Nothing to install. Nothing to configure. Just:

- A browser, on a computer or a phone
- A free account on one AI tool
- About ten minutes for your first build

That's the whole list.

---

## 🔧 Pick One Tool

Any of these work. If you don't already have a preference, start with **Claude** or **Gemini** — both are free, open in a browser, and need nothing installed:

| Tool | What it's good for |
|---|---|
| **Claude** (claude.ai) | General-purpose, great at explaining what it built |
| **Gemini** (gemini.google.com) | Widely available, works well for this too |
| **Replit / Lovable / Bolt** | Purpose-built for going straight from idea to running app |

A free account is enough for everything in this course. Free tiers do have limits — if a tool ever slows down, asks you to wait, or caps how much you can send in a day, that's the free tier working as designed, not something you broke. Waiting it out or switching tools for that session both work fine.

## 🧭 The One Trick for When a Button Isn't Where We Said

Every lesson in this course describes actions in plain language — "find the preview," "look for a publish button" — because that's genuinely all you need to know *what* to do. But these tools update their interfaces constantly, and no lesson can promise a button will be in the exact spot or have the exact label it has today.

<Pitfall title="Can't Find Something a Lesson Mentions? Ask the AI.">

If you're ever looking for a button, tab, or menu a lesson describes and it isn't where you expect, don't hunt around guessing. Just ask, in the same chat:

> "Where do I click to see this running?" or "How do I get a shareable link to this?"

The AI knows its own current interface better than any lesson written in advance ever could. This isn't a workaround — it's the same Directing skill Module 1 teaches, aimed at the tool itself instead of your app.

</Pitfall>
