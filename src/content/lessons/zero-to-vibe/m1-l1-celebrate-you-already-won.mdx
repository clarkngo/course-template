## 🎉 Celebrate: You Already Won

The guiding idea for this module — and the whole course:

**You get to feel like a builder before you understand how building works.**

In the very next lesson, before any lecture or vocabulary, you'll build a real, working app that runs in a browser. Understanding *why* it works comes after — this is the flipped Bloom's Taxonomy model from Module 0, in motion for the first time.

> The celebration moment of Module 1: **you don't have to earn the win. You get to have it first, and then understand it.**
